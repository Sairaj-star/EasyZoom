Can't join in your browser?

Zoom may open the app or ask you to install it. Joining in a browser depends on the host's settings and Zoom's support for your device.

WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Let's do it together.

Choose the sentence that best describes your situation.

I received a meeting link on WhatsApp
  1. Open WhatsApp and find the message.
  2. Press and hold the meeting link, then choose Copy.
  3. Return to EasyZoom and tap Join my meeting.
  4. Choose I have a link, paste it, then tap Open my meeting.
I have a meeting ID and passcode
  1. Tap Join my meeting.
  2. Choose I have a meeting ID.
  3. Enter the meeting ID and passcode from your invitation.
  4. Tap Continue to Zoom and follow any prompts.
I need to install Zoom
  1. Tap Install Zoom.
  2. Tap the official download button.
  3. Follow the instructions on your device or app store. You may need to confirm installation.
  4. Return to EasyZoom when you're ready to join.
Zoom asks me to open the app or sign in

Follow the instructions Zoom shows. Some meetings require the app, sign-in, a passcode, or permission to use your camera and microphone. EasyZoom cannot bypass those requirements.
